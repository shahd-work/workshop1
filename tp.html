About Her

Marie Curie was a Polish-born physicist and chemist who conducted pioneering research on radioactivity She was the first woman to win a Nobel Prize, and the only person to win Nobel Prizes in two different scientific fields.

Work

Marie Curie's work on radioactivity led to the development of new medical treatments and technologies, including X-rays and cancer therapies. She also discovered two new elements, polonium and radium, and developed techniques for isolating radioactive isotopes.

Resume

Marie Curie's resume includes numerous awards and honors, including the Nobel Prize in Physics (1903) and the Nobel Prize in Chemistry (1911). She also founded the Radium Institute in Paris, which became a leading center for research on radioactivity.

  • Nobel Prize in Physics, 1903 (shared)
  • Nobel Prize in Chemistry, 1911
